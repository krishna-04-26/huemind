How HueMind Works

DistilBERT fine-tuned on 16,000 labelled sentences — real metrics, real results.

🤖

DistilBERT Base

66M-parameter distilled BERT transformer. 6 layers, 12 attention heads, 768-dim hidden states. 40% smaller, 60% faster than BERT-Base with 97% of its language understanding.

🎯

Fine-Tuning

Classification head (768 to 6) added above the [CLS] token. Cross-entropy loss, AdamW optimiser, 3 epochs on the DAIR.AI Emotion dataset with linear LR warmup.

📊

Dataset

DAIR.AI Emotion — English Twitter messages labelled with six emotions. 16,000 training · 2,000 validation · 2,000 test examples.

⚡

Inference

Single forward pass through the model. Softmax applied to raw logits yields calibrated probabilities for all six classes simultaneously.

Training Data Distribution

Class balance in the 16,000-example training split. Sadness and joy dominate; surprise is the minority class — explaining its lower F1.

About

Built on real science.

HueMind is a demonstration of transfer learning applied to affective computing. DistilBERT was originally trained on Books Corpus and Wikipedia; fine-tuning on labelled emotion data teaches it to map subtle linguistic patterns — word choice, punctuation, sentence rhythm — to emotional states.

The model runs on CPU-compatible PyTorch, served through a FastAPI backend. Predictions are made in a single forward pass, with no data stored or logged beyond the session.
